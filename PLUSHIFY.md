# Plushify - Project Bible

## The Vision
Launch $PLUSH as a meme coin with a real destination:
- Build a community that believes The Plushifier can flip the ATH market cap of Toys R Us
- Eventually operate a real online store: submit any photo (PFP, avatar, portrait) → receive an exact plush replica + a 1/1 NFT
- Think: Build-A-Bear meets crypto PFP culture meets the world's most ambitious toy factory

## Token
- **Name:** Plushify
- **Ticker:** $PLUSH
- **Platform:** Pump.fun

## The Mission / Community Hook
"Flip Toys R Us." Simple. Audacious. Culturally resonant.
- Toys R Us ATH market cap: ~$11B (before LBO/bankruptcy)
- That's the target. The community's moonshot.
- Narrative: the internet killed Toys R Us. Now the internet is going to build something bigger.

## NFT Minting Rules
- 1/1 NFT minting goes live once $PLUSH hits $10M market cap
- Until then, winners get their plush made physically but NFT mint is held
- When MC hits $10M, Kobe drops the treasury wallet keypair and minting goes live
- Admin panel has the mint button — stays disabled until the $10M milestone

## Community Gallery & Voting
- After generating a plush preview, users can submit it to the community library
- Community votes on submissions (upvotes)
- Daily or weekly winner gets made into a real 1/1 physical plush by the design team
- Winner also receives a 1/1 NFT
- Creates daily engagement, reason to share, and a curated queue for the plush designers
- Small team of plush designers fulfills winners first, scales up over time

## The Product (end state)
- Online store run by The Plushifier (the AI)
- Submit any photo → get an exact plush replica made to order
- Every plush comes with a 1/1 NFT (tied to the physical item)
- Especially powerful for crypto PFP culture (Apes, Punks, any avatar)
- "Your PFP. But soft."
